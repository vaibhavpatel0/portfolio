AI · Consumer safety

SafeByte

AI-based label compliance checker

Live demo GitHub

A web application that analyses photographs of packaged food and medicine labels and checks them against Indian labelling regulations. The system first identifies the type of product, then extracts and verifies the required declarations.

  • Checks 12 mandatory declarations on food labels under the Legal Metrology and FSS Labelling rules, including MRP, net quantity, expiry date, batch number and allergen information.
  • Verifies FSSAI licence numbers against the government FoSCoS registry and confirms that the licence belongs to the company named on the package.
  • Performs 15 checks on medicine labels under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, including identification of Schedule H, H1 and X drugs and their required warnings.
  • Supports 11 Indian languages with voice input and output, and prepares complaint details for submission on the FoSCoS portal.
Technologies

JavaScript, Node.js, HTML, CSS, Gemini, Groq, Mistral and OpenRouter APIs, Web Speech API

Full-stack · Multi-tenant

NotifyHub

College communication platform

Live site GitHub

A multi-tenant web platform that provides each participating college with its own digital notice board. Verified college administrators publish notices, events and announcements, and students can view them without creating an account.

  • Serves every college from a single application, each on its own subdomain, with data separated using PostgreSQL Row Level Security.
  • Verifies college ownership through an official email address, a DNS record or a website tag before a portal is published.
  • Supports college-level and department-level administrators, with notices targeted by year and department.
  • Delivers new notices in real time, and includes urgent pinned notices, scheduled publishing, automatic expiry, attachments and search.
Technologies

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime, Storage), Vercel
